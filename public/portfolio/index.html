LIVE / AOW WEBSITE

A business site with a terminal look

The brief: Give AOW a recognizable home online and make it easy for customers to understand the services and get in touch.

What’s built: A responsive five-page site with keyboard-friendly navigation, switchable accent colors, a skippable retro intro, and service-specific quote links. The lightweight site is hosted independently of a home computer.

Try it: Pick a service. The quote link takes you to the matching option on the Contact page, where you can open a prefilled email draft.
