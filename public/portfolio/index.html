LIVE CASE STUDY / AOW’S OWN WEBSITE

From a dead-end form to a usable quote path

The first version of this site had a Contact form preview visitors couldn’t submit. The rebuild kept the terminal look and gave each service a clear path to an email draft, with a copyable template for visitors who prefer webmail.

View the original Contact page source on GitHub. The current flow opens a draft in your email app or lets you copy text; the site itself does not send email.

What a small project like this could cover

  • Trace how someone gets from a service page to an inquiry and identify where they get stuck.
  • Replace dead ends with a clear next step, including a fallback for visitors using webmail.
  • Check the revised path on small screens and with keyboard navigation.
