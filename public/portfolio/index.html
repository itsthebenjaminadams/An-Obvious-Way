Read a worked case: the back room

In the same fictional incident, a second device also drops on the same Wi-Fi in the back room. The affected laptop is stable near the router and is also stable when wired with Wi-Fi off.

What to investigate first: Wireless coverage or interference in the back room. Both devices struggle in that spot, while moving the laptop or bypassing Wi-Fi helps. That is a lead, not proof of what caused the drops.
