aow / philosophy

Start with what needs to work

Sometimes the best fix is small: a clearer contact page, a device setting, or a cleaner spreadsheet. We start with what’s getting in the way and look for a solution that’s useful and straightforward to maintain.

/ how we work

What it’s like to work with us

  1. Tell us the goal.Explain what isn’t working or what you want to build.

    Start with the outcome

    A rough description is enough to start. For example, tell us when the Wi-Fi drops or what’s missing from your current website.

  2. Review the scope and quote.Decide whether to proceed once you know the proposed work and cost.

    Agree on a practical next step

    We’ll clarify the scope and cost. If the cause isn’t clear, start with a diagnostic rather than assuming a bigger repair.

  3. Understand the handoff.Know what changed and what you’ll need to maintain.

    Make the result usable

    For a fix or build, the handoff explains what changed, how to use it, and what to maintain.
