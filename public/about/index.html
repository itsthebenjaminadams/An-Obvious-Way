/ about

A simpler way to get things done.

An Obvious Way (AOW) is an independent technology business offering tech support, websites, and workflow automation for individuals and small businesses.

aow / philosophy

Why bring these services together?

Small technology problems rarely stay in one lane. A website can be running fine while customers still can’t find the contact button. A weekly report might need a cleaner spreadsheet before it needs a script. We start by working out what’s in the way, then choose a fix that suits the problem and is straightforward to maintain.

/ how we work

From first question to handoff

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the outcome

Tell us what you’re trying to do, what’s getting in the way, and what a good result would look like. You don’t need to know the technical fix.

02 / SCOPE

Agree on a practical next step

We narrow down what to tackle, what can wait, and what’s included in the quote. That might mean diagnosing one issue before planning a larger change.

03 / HANDOFF

Make the result usable

The goal is a fix or build you understand: what changed, how to use it, and what you may need to maintain after the work.

Curious what that looks like?

The site you’re browsing is one example: a terminal-inspired design with ordinary navigation and direct links to request a quote.
